## Who is CARE-E

CARE-E listens to patient encounters, captures structured notes, generates documentation, manages orders, and suggests treatment plans while maintaining accurate and consistent patient records. Beyond documentation, CARE-E streamlines repetitive tasks, while providing clinical decision support (CDS), real-time insights, and analytics to assist clinicians in making informed decisions at the point of care. Clinicians review and approve all information before it’s updated in the EHR, ensuring accuracy and reliability.

By integrating seamlessly with existing EHRs–CARE-E joins you throughout your workday, as a smart layer to your system.

### Tab 1: Clinical Documentation

CARE-E automates repetitive administrative tasks, such as appointment scheduling, reminders, and data entry, allowing clinicians to spend more time with patients. Key processes include:

- ![](https://web.archive.org/web/20260417185411im_/https://care-e.ai/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/automated-process-1.png) Automated Structured Note Generation – Transforms clinician inputs, voice dictation, and patient interactions into well-organized, structured clinical notes in real-time.
- ![](https://web.archive.org/web/20260417185411im_/https://care-e.ai/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/24-hours-1.png) Real-Time Assistance – Captures relevant clinical details during encounters, ensuring immediate, precise documentation and reducing the need for retrospective corrections.
- ![](https://web.archive.org/web/20260417185411im_/https://care-e.ai/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/execution-1.png) Comprehensive Medical Summaries – Prepares detailed summaries by analyzing past documentation, automatically compiling past medical history (PMH), past surgical history (PSH), history of present illness (HPI), orders, treatment plans, visit summary and patient summaries to ensure accurate, up-to-date records.
