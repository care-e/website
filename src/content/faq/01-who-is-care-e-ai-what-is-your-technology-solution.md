CARE-E Ai is a leading provider of AI-driven clinical automation solutions for healthcare. Our flagship technology, CARE-E, is an EHR-integrated AI assistant that streamlines administrative workflows, reduces clinician burnout, and enhances operational efficiency. CARE-E automates manual tasks such as documentation and referrals, while enabling providers to focus on patient care while improving data accuracy and compliance.
